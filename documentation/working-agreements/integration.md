# Team Working Agreement

**Team:** Integration
**Members:** Daoming Zhang, Meshaal Aldawood Github: Daoming-Zhang, meshaalaldawood
**Date agreed:** 09/22/2026

## Meetings and availability

- Our weekly check-in with the advisor is: Wednesday 3:00-3:30 pm
- Our team meets (beyond the check-in): 1x/week, Monday 8:00 pm, video
- Our core overlap hours (when everyone, including distance members, can be
  reached live) are: weekdays after 3:00 pm
- Outside overlap hours, we expect responses within: 24 hours on weekdays

## Communication

- Day-to-day discussion happens in: Slack/texts/WhatsApp
- Decisions and their reasons are recorded in: posts in Slack bug26-integrators channel
  so that anyone who missed a live conversation can catch up without asking.
- Work-in-progress questions go to: GitHub issues/Slack

## How we make decisions

- For routine decisions: whoever does the work decides, documented in the PR
- For decisions that affect interfaces with other teams or are hard to reverse:
  discussed at team meeting/Slack
- If we can't agree: timebox debate to one meeting, then vote; ask advisor

## How we handle being stuck

- Per the three-day rule, anyone blocked for three days must ask for help.
- Before that, we check in on blockers: a quick zoom call/meetup to see if we can work it out
- If a teammate goes quiet, we: reach out directly first, then raise with the team

## Work and credit

- We divide work by: volunteering at planning, rotating unpopular tasks
- Pull requests are reviewed by: at least one other member within 72 hours
- If someone's circumstances change (illness, interviews, workload), they tell the
  team as early as possible, and we rebalance rather than resent.

## Feedback

- We give each other kind, honest feedback: a 10-minute retrospective at the end of each week's meeting
- Peer evaluations should never contain surprises. If something would show up
  in a peer evaluation, we say it to each other first.
